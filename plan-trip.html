Plan Your Trip

A weekend route across Albay and Camarines Sur — from Legazpi's Mayon viewpoints to the lagoons of Caramoan — plus everything you need to pack, prepare, and ask us along the way.

01 — Itinerary

Three days · Two provinces

A weekend route across Bicol.

Built around the most reliable flight route — Manila to Legazpi (1 hr). Add a fourth day if you want to extend to Caramoan overnight.

3-day itinerary Bicol weekend — Legazpi → Caramoan → Donsol
Time Day 1 — Arrival & Mayon Day 2 — Caramoan Islands Day 3 — Donsol & Departure
Morning07:00 – 11:00 Arrive Legazpi (RGN airport) Pick up transfer, check-in to hotel in Legazpi City. Breakfast at Smalltalk Cafe — order the Bicol Express pasta. Drive to Sabang port 2-hour van from Legazpi to Sabang, then a 30-minute banca to Caramoan. Lunch at the host resort before launch. Drive to Donsol 2.5-hour transfer to Donsol, Sorsogon. Register at the Butanding Interaction Center for the 7 am slot.
Afternoon11:00 – 16:00 Cagsawa Ruins & ATV Visit the bell-tower ruins, then a 2-hour ATV circuit on the 2006 Mayon lava flow. Snack at the abaca weavers' stalls. Island-hopping circuit Banca tour of Matukad, Lahos, and Pitogo islands — kayak through sea caves and swim in lagoons open at low tide. Whale-shark interaction Snorkel-only encounter with butanding. WWF-regulated, max 6 swimmers per shark, no touching, no flash.
Evening16:00 – 21:00 Ligñon Hill sunset Drive up Ligñon Hill for the sunset view of Mayon's silhouette. Dinner at Balay Suanoy — try the pinangat and pili tart. Resort dinner & rest Quiet dinner at the Caramoan resort; pack a day-bag for tomorrow's drive to Donsol. Firefly river tour 6 pm banca ride up the Donsol River mangrove corridor — thousands of fireflies light the trees like Christmas.

02 — Tips & packing

Practical advice

Ten tips and a checklist.

Travel tips

  • 01

    Fly into Legazpi (LGP) Manila to Legazpi is a one-hour flight on Cebu Pacific or Philippine Airlines — daily, often under PHP 2,500 round-trip if booked two weeks ahead.

  • 02

    Travel between October and May The dry season offers the clearest views of Mayon and the calmest seas for Caramoan. Avoid the typhoon months of July–September.

  • 03

    Book the whale-shark slot the day before Donsol's Butanding Center issues 25 boat permits per day. Register in person at 4 pm for the next morning's 7 am slot.

  • 04

    Carry cash ATMs are reliable in Legazpi and Naga but scarce in Caramoan and Donsol. Withdraw PHP 5,000–10,000 before leaving the cities.

  • 05

    Bring reef-safe sunscreen Oxybenzone-free sunscreen is required for any swim near the Caramoan reefs. Available at Watsons in Legazpi.

  • 06

    Hire a guide for Mt. Isarog Permits and a registered guide are required at the PENRO office in Naga. Book at least one day ahead — the trailhead is 1.5 hours from the city.

  • 07

    Respect the chilies Bicolano dishes are spicier than they look. Ask for "less spicy" (halang hinabo) — most kitchens will accommodate.

  • 08

    Use tricycles for short hops, vans for cities Tricycle fares within Legazpi are PHP 10–50. For Naga or CamSur, book a UV Express van — fixed routes, PHP 60–120/seat.

  • 09

    Bring a dry bag Almost every activity involves getting wet — banca rides, kayaks, whale-shark swims. A 10-litre dry bag costs PHP 400 at SM Legazpi.

  • 10

    Save your receipts Most resorts in Caramoan and Donsol are cash-only and tax-free for foreign tourists on stays of 4+ nights — keep receipts for refund claims at the airport.
