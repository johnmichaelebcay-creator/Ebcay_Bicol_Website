Region V · Six provinces

Popular Destinations

From the perfect cone of Mayon to the hidden lagoons of Caramoan, here are six of the most rewarding stops across Albay, Camarines Sur, and Sorsogon.

№ 01 · Stratovolcano

Mayon Volcano

Daraga & Legazpi · Albay

Rising 2,463 metres from the Albay plain, Mayon is the world's most perfectly conical volcano — a near-symmetrical silhouette visible from almost anywhere in the province. It is also the country's most active, with recorded eruptions dating to 1616. The summit crater glows faintly at night during periods of unrest, and the surrounding national park offers viewpoints, lava trails, and ATV routes across old flow fields.

№ 02 · Ruins & Park

Cagsawa Ruins

Daraga · Albay

The Cagsawa church was buried in 1814 when Mayon erupted violently, killing more than a thousand residents who had taken shelter inside. Today, only the freestanding baroque bell tower remains above the lava line — one of the most photographed ruins in the country. The park around it hosts craft stalls, an abaca-weaving demonstration area, and the clearest unobstructed view of Mayon anywhere in Albay.

№ 03 · Archipelago

Caramoan Islands

Caramoan Peninsula · Camarines Sur

A scatter of limestone islands ringed by hidden lagoons and powder-fine sand — long closed to mass tourism and reachable only by boat. The international Survivor franchise filmed multiple seasons here, yet most islands remain stunningly empty on weekdays. Sea caves, snorkel reefs, and tide-dependent lagoons are explored by kayak or outrigger banca from Sabang port.

№ 04 · Marine sanctuary

Donsol Whale Sharks

Donsol · Sorsogon

From February to May, plankton blooms draw the world's largest fish — the whale shark, locally called butanding — to feed in the shallow waters off Donsol. The interaction here is strictly snorkel-only, regulated by the World Wildlife Fund, and is among the most reliable and ethical whale-shark encounters on earth. Donsol River also hosts evening firefly tours through mangrove corridors.

№ 05 · Cable wakepark

CamSur Watersports Complex

Pili · Camarines Sur

A six-hectare cable-ski complex inside the provincial capitol grounds, CWC pioneered cable wakeboarding in Southeast Asia. A continuous overhead cable tows riders around an artificial lagoon without need of a boat — making it one of the most accessible wakeparks in the world for first-timers and pros alike. On-site cabanas, a skateboard park, and a 1.8 km running loop round out the campus.

№ 06 · Beach resort

Misibis Bay

Bacacay · Albay

A private five-hectare resort on Cagraray Island facing the Pacific, reachable by a 20-minute speedboat from Legazpi. Misibis offers white-sand coves, an infinity pool on the cliffside, and adventure circuits that include a zip-line over the canopy, an ATV trail to the hilltop Peace Chapel, and a mangrove boardwalk — all within a single gated estate.
